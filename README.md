Subham Flag Checklist — Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)This guide explains how to fill out, preview, submit, and reset the daily Subham Flag Checklist in Google Sheets.   1. One-Time Setup (For New Users)Before using the checklist for the first time, grant the script permission to access Google Sheets and Google Drive:   Open the checklist Google Sheet and click 🏳Subham Flag Checklist in the top menu bar.   Click 🔐Authorize Script.   Follow the Google prompts to allow permissions.   Once complete, a green Authorization Complete popup will confirm your email, the form spreadsheet, the Responses file, and the Flag Checklist Pdf folder.   2. How to Fill Out the ChecklistOpen the SubhamFlagCheckListForm tab and complete the required fields. The system will block submission or PDF preview if the form is blank or missing mandatory details:   Date: Enter the date in C11 (or in the signature date cells H36:H38).   Shift Selection: Tick E13 for AM Shift, I13 for PM Shift, or both.   Inspection Checkpoints (Rows 17–25): Tick OK / FAIL (and Yes / No for Defect Reported in Row 25) for your active shift:   AM Shift: Columns E and G (Rows 17–25).   PM Shift: Columns I and K (Rows 17–25, including Night Lighting in Row 24).   Guard Name / Signature:AM Shift: Fill in Morning Guard Name (C12) or AM Signature (C36).   PM Shift: Fill in Evening Guard Name (E12) or PM Signature (C37).   Additional Details (as needed): Fill in Location/Mast ID (E11), Supervisor (C13), Defect details (C28:C32, H28:H31), and signature times (E36:E38).   3. Preview & Print PDF (Optional)To check how the form looks or print a copy without saving it to Google Drive:   Click 🏳Subham Flag Checklist → 👁‍🗨Preview & Print PDF.   The script validates your entries and opens an A4 Portrait PDF preview (covering cells B1:L43) in a new browser tab.   Print or close the tab—no file is created in Drive during preview.   4. Submit the ChecklistWhen the inspection is complete:Click 🏳Subham Flag Checklist → ✔Submit Checklist.   The script automatically performs the following steps:   Validates that all mandatory fields are filled.   Generates a unique Submission ID (format: SUB-yyyyMMdd-HHmmss).   Saves a PDF copy (Subham_Flag_Checklist_<SUB-ID>.pdf) to the Flag Checklist Pdf folder in Google Drive.   Logs all checklist answers as a new row in the Responses sheet, with the Submission ID in Column A hyperlinked directly to the saved PDF.   Wait for the green Checklist Submitted! popup showing your Submission ID, Shift, and Response Row number.   5. Reset the Form for the Next ShiftAfter submitting, clear the form so it is ready for the next entry:   Click 🏳Subham Flag Checklist → 🔄Reset Form.   Click Yes on the confirmation prompt.   All checkboxes (E13, I13, E17:E25, G17:G25, I17:I25, K17:K25) are unchecked and all text/date fields (C11:C13, E11:E12, C28:C32, H28:H31, C36:C38, E36:E38, H36:H38) are cleared.   6. Quick TroubleshootingPopup MessageWhat It MeansWhat to DoValidation Required / Cannot Generate PDF   One or more mandatory fields are empty.   Fill in the specific cells listed in the amber popup and try again.   System Busy   Another user is submitting at the exact same time.   Wait a few seconds and click Submit Checklist again.   Access Error / Authorization Failed   Your Google account lacks permission to the Responses sheet or Drive folder.   Run 🔐Authorize Script again or ask the admin to share the destination sheet/folder with you.   Worksheet Missing   A required tab was renamed or deleted.   Ensure the tabs are named SubhamFlagCheckListForm and Responses.   Project Files Overviewcode.js — Stores sheet/folder IDs and creates the custom 🏳Subham Flag Checklist menu.
